Account & Data Deletion

Applicable to: Blackjack 6-Deck Table, Calm Chess, and associated applications

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

At Calm Chess Computers, we respect your right to control your personal data. In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal information at any time.

Account deletion is permanent. Once completed, your credentials will be deactivated, and your saved in-game chips, stats, and profile cannot be recovered.

2. Steps to Request Account & Data Deletion

Users can request account and data deletion through any of the following convenient methods:

Method A: Online Web Request Form (Immediate)

Use the encrypted self-service deletion form below on this page. Enter the username or email address associated with your Calm Chess Computers account and submit your request.

Method B: In-App Deletion (Mobile or Web)
  1. Open the Blackjack 6-Deck Table application on your Android device or browser.
  2. Ensure you are signed in with your account credentials.
  3. Navigate to the Account Settings or table menu.
  4. Select "Delete Account & Purge Data" and confirm your choice.
Method C: Direct Email Request

Send an email from your registered email address to bberry@calmchessgames.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Your request will be processed within 24 to 48 business hours.

4. Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

When an account deletion request is processed, our systems categorize and handle user data according to the schedule below:

Data Category Specific Items Action Taken Retention Period
Account Credentials Email address, username, salted bcrypt password hash Permanently Purged Deleted within 24–48 hours
Gameplay & Stats Virtual bankroll, chips balance, game level, hand logs, AI strategy hint history Permanently Purged Deleted within 24–48 hours
Session Data JWT authentication tokens, browser sessions, local storage keys Revoked & Purged Immediately terminated
Financial & Purchase Ledgers Google Play Order IDs, PayPal transaction tokens, timestamps, amount paid Retained (Pseudonymized) Retained for up to 7 years to comply with statutory accounting, tax laws, and anti-fraud/chargeback obligations. All personal identifiers (name, email) are stripped and detached from the ledger entry upon account deletion.

5. Processing Timeline & Data Retention Periods

  • Account Deactivation: Immediate upon receipt of your verified request. The account can no longer be accessed or logged into.
  • Permanent Data Purge: All personal credentials, contact information, and gameplay records are permanently deleted from active production databases within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Database Backups: Residual encrypted backups are cycled and permanently purged according to our automated 30-day backup retention rotation.
  • Statutory Retention: Anonymized transaction records required under applicable commercial and tax regulations are kept for up to 7 years without any link to your identity.
