Privacy Policy & Cookies Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Calm Chess Computers • Brian Berry

Summary: Calm Chess Computers respects your digital privacy. We collect only the minimal data required for account authentication, guest trials, and game state. We use strictly essential cookies for secure session management and never sell, broker, or share personal data with third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization principles. The personal information collected depends on how you interact with our platform:

  • Registered Account Information: Email address (for account recovery and login identification), optional chosen username, and a cryptographic Bcrypt hash of your password. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Guest & Trial Play Information: When playing as a guest, a randomized anonymous identifier (e.g. guest_<id>) and a PHP session token are generated. No email, name, or phone number is collected for guest trials.
  • In-Game Preferences & State: Game settings, board configurations, sound preferences, and session state are saved to provide continuous gameplay.
  • In-App Purchase & Financial Ledger: For transactions processed via PayPal or Google Play In-App Billing, we record order IDs, product SKU, purchase timestamp, credits awarded, and payment method to fulfill orders and comply with statutory 7-year financial accounting and tax retention regulations (IRS compliance). Payment card details are processed exclusively by PayPal or Google Play and are never received or stored on our servers.
  • Consultation & Contact Requests: When submitting appointment requests or contact inquiries, we collect attendee name, email address, optional phone number, and consultation topic solely to schedule and conduct the requested meeting.

2. Cookies & Local Storage Policy

We use cookies and browser local storage strictly for essential operations, session continuity, and user preferences. We do not use third-party tracking, behavioral advertising, or data-broker cookies.

Cookie / Storage Name Type Standard Duration Purpose & Description
PHPSESSID Essential / First-Party Session Secures your authenticated session, maintains essential application state, and prevents cross-site request forgery.
cc_admin_jwt Security / SessionStorage Session (cleared on tab close) HMAC-SHA256 authenticated administrative authorization token for authorized maintenance.
calmchess_segments Functional / LocalStorage Persistent Caches navigation ring layouts and interactive board configurations for rapid rendering.
cc_sound_pref Functional / LocalStorage Persistent Remembers your audio mute/unmute preferences across gameplay sessions.

All session cookies are configured with strict security flags: SameSite=Lax or SameSite=Strict, HttpOnly (blocking JavaScript credential theft), and Secure (mandating TLS/HTTPS transport).

You may configure your web browser to reject cookies; however, doing so will prevent you from staying signed in, preserving preferences, or accessing authenticated gaming features.

3. In-Transit Encryption & Security Standards

We employ multi-layer security protections to safeguard your data:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3): All network traffic between your client and our backend is encrypted via HTTPS with modern cipher suites.
  • Application-Layer Encryption (AES-GCM): Sensitive API payloads (including authentication and checkout verification) are encrypted prior to network transmission using our client-side cryptographic transport library.
  • Prepared SQL Statements: All database queries use parameterized prepared statements with strict type binding to eliminate SQL injection vectors.

4. Account & Data Deletion (GDPR & Google Play Compliance)

You have the absolute right to request permanent deletion of your account credentials and personal data at any time. To exercise this right:

  • Visit our automated Account Deletion Request Page to submit an immediate deletion request.
  • Alternatively, email your request directly to bberry@calmchessgames.com.

Personal credentials, emails, and active game states will be permanently purged within 24–48 hours. In accordance with statutory tax and financial accounting requirements, financial transaction ledger rows are retained in a pseudonymized, anonymized form (with user IDs removed and emails replaced with audit placeholders).
