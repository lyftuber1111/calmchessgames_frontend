How to Play

  • Five Tall Mystery Boxes stand on pedestals, each paired with its own Tall Spring-Loaded Clicker.
  • Click or tap any tall clicker plunger (or press 1–5 or Space) to pump mystery energy up the tall tower and crack open its vault lid.
  • Listen closely! Most clicks emit sneaky decoy sounds (boings, quacks, chimes, meows). One secret Tall Mystery Box hides the Golden Cow.
  • The moment you hear a MOO, the tall mystery box erupts with the Golden Cow and flying $1,000 notes — and you win $1,000!
  • In VR / AR (WebXR), point your controller ray or pinch at any tall clicker or box to press it, and press the floating 3D Next Round button after a jackpot.
  • Drag to orbit the 3D camera, scroll/pinch to zoom, press R to shuffle a new round, or M to mute/unmute.

Game winnings are virtual in-game prize dollars saved locally in your browser.
